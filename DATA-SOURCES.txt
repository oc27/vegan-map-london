Map methodology and caveats:
- All 44 entries have a latitude and longitude and therefore appear as map pins.
- Coordinates are approximate street/neighbourhood positions, not surveyed entrance coordinates. Do not rely on them for precise navigation.
- Some businesses operate multiple branches. Where the listing represents a brand rather than one branch, one representative London branch is pinned and the popup says so.
- Some entries are temporary/mobile/pop-up listings. Fed By Plants is marked at Windrush Square based on recent 2026 posts; check the Instagram account for current trading location.
- Chickenish and LD's Kitchen are pinned at their reported Saint Monday residency; check current residency/service dates.
- Carb Club appeared in the source list but is a creative workshop listing rather than a food venue. It is retained and clearly labelled as such rather than silently removed.
- Vegan status and operating details may change. This is a community guide, not an independently verified directory or endorsement. Visitors should check directly with each venue before visiting.

Map rendering:
- Coordinates are bundled in data/venues.json. The browser does not geocode addresses or call Nominatim.
- Leaflet and OpenStreetMap tiles still require an internet connection.
